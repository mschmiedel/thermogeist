# Frost protection (central mode)

**English** | [Deutsch](../../de/faq/frost_protection.md)

## What you see

Active reason: **Frost protection (central mode)**

## What it means

The central mode of the house is set to *Frost protection*. Every room only keeps 7 °C so that nothing freezes – useful when you are away for longer.

## What to check

1. Look at **Central mode** on the house device.

## What to do

- Set the central mode back to *Auto* when you are home again. Plan for some hours until rooms are warm again, especially with a heat pump.
