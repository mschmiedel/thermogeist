# Window open

**English** | [Deutsch](../../de/faq/window_open.md)

## What you see

Active reason: **Window open** (attribute `open_minutes`)

## What it means

A window or door contact of the room has been open for at least 30 seconds. The room aims for frost protection (7 °C) and, if controlled, closes the valve at once.

## What to check

1. Is a window or door really open?
2. Does the contact report correctly (battery, position of the magnet)?

## What to do

- Close the window. The room returns to its setpoint immediately, with the valve where it was before.
