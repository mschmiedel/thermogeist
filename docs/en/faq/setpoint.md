# Holding the setpoint

**English** | [Deutsch](../../de/faq/setpoint.md)

## What you see

Active reason: **Holding the setpoint**

## What it means

Everything is normal. The room aims for the temperature you set on its climate entity.

## What to check

1. Nothing to check.

## What to do

- Nothing to do. Change the target temperature on the room if you want it warmer or cooler.
