# Waiting after restart

**English** | [Deutsch](../../de/faq/startup_hold.md)

## What you see

Active reason: **Waiting after restart**

## What it means

Home Assistant was started less than 90 seconds ago. Thermogeist waits so that all devices are available before it writes anything.

## What to check

1. Nothing to check.

## What to do

- Nothing to do; it ends by itself.
