# Heating stopped (central mode)

**English** | [Deutsch](../../de/faq/central_stop.md)

## What you see

Active reason: **Heating stopped (central mode)**

## What it means

The central mode of the house is set to *Stop*. No room heats, whatever its own setpoint is.

## What to check

1. Look at **Central mode** on the house device.
2. Check automations that might set the central mode, e.g. a summer automation.

## What to do

- Set the central mode back to *Auto* when heating should resume.
